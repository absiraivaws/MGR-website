About Mannar Green Ride

Promoting Eco-Tourism & Active Travel in Mannar

Mannar Green Ride was established to bridge local vehicle owners with international visitors and fitness-minded travelers exploring Sri Lanka's unique northwestern coastline.

We promote clean transportation, encourage healthy lifestyle habits through fitness cycling, and empower local families by coordinating idle cars, vans, buses, and two-wheelers through our verified community host network.
